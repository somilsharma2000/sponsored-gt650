## SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT

**Campaign:** The Sponsored GT650 — A 30-Day Public Marketing Experiment

**Campaign Owner:** [Your Name / Business Entity Name]

**Sponsor:** [Brand Name]

**Date:** [Date]

**Position:** [Position Label, e.g., "CROWN" or "SPOT #06"]

**Tier:** [Crown / Founding / Standard / Community]

**Amount:** ₹[Amount]

---

### 1. SCOPE OF SPONSORSHIP

The Campaign Owner agrees to place the Sponsor's brand logo/design on the designated position on the GT650 lid for a minimum period of 12 months from the date of GT650 purchase.

### 2. SPONSOR DELIVERABLES

The Sponsor will receive:
1. Physical brand placement on the GT650 lid at the designated position
2. A unique QR code linked to the Sponsor's website
3. A dedicated sponsor profile page on the campaign website
4. Social media content (quantity based on tier):
   - Crown: 4 posts (X, LinkedIn, Instagram)
   - Founding: 2 posts (X, LinkedIn)
   - Standard: 1 post (X or LinkedIn)
   - Community: 1 mention
5. Inclusion in the published case study
6. Access to live QR scan and click analytics

### 3. PAYMENT TERMS

1. The Sponsor agrees to pay the full amount within 7 days of receiving the invoice via Razorpay.
2. Payment methods: UPI, credit/debit card, net banking.
3. An invoice will be issued upon payment.
4. If payment is not received within 7 days, the position will be released back to the market.

### 4. REFUND POLICY

1. If the campaign does not reach its ₹6,00,000 target within 30 days, the Sponsor is entitled to a 100% refund.
2. Refunds will be processed within 7 business days of the campaign end date.
3. Alternatively, the Sponsor may choose to roll the sponsorship forward to Campaign #2 at the same price.
4. After the GT650 is purchased, refunds are not available as the funds have been spent on the product.

### 5. BRAND GUIDELINES

1. The Sponsor will provide a logo/design in vector format (SVG, AI, or EPS) at minimum 300 DPI.
2. The Campaign Owner reserves the right to resize and adapt the logo for physical placement.
3. The Campaign Owner will not alter the Sponsor's logo in any way that misrepresents the brand.
4. Offensive, illegal, or misleading logos will not be accepted.

### 6. CONTENT RIGHTS

1. The Sponsor grants the Campaign Owner the right to use their brand name and logo in campaign-related content, including:
   - Social media posts
   - The campaign website
   - The published case study
   - Press materials
2. The Campaign Owner grants the Sponsor the right to share campaign content featuring their brand.
3. Neither party may use the other's brand in ways not related to this campaign without written consent.

### 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

1. The GT650 and all physical branding materials remain the property of the Campaign Owner.
2. The Sponsor retains all rights to their logo, brand name, and trademarks.
3. The campaign concept and case study remain the intellectual property of the Campaign Owner.

### 8. LIABILITY

1. The Campaign Owner is not liable for any business outcomes (sales, signups, revenue) resulting from the sponsorship.
2. The Campaign Owner will make reasonable efforts to display and promote the GT650 at public events.
3. The Campaign Owner is not liable for loss, theft, or damage to the GT650.
4. The Sponsor agrees that the value of this sponsorship is the brand placement, content, and case study inclusion — not guaranteed impressions or conversions.

### 9. TERMINATION

1. Either party may terminate this agreement before the GT650 is purchased, subject to the refund policy in Section 4.
2. After the GT650 is purchased, the sponsorship is active for 12 months and cannot be terminated.
3. If the Sponsor breaches any terms, the Campaign Owner may remove the Sponsor's branding without refund.

### 10. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes will be resolved in [City], India.
