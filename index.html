Questions, Answered

Is this real? ▾
100%. Every position, bid, and rupee is tracked in a public database. The bike gets purchased on camera when the goal is hit, and the whole journey — wins, losses, rejections — is published as a 12-month case study.
What does a sponsor actually get? ▾
A premium vinyl placement on the bike (not a sticker), a unique QR code linking to your site with live scan analytics, a sponsor profile page, category exclusivity (no competitor on the bike), social content features, and inclusion in every documented tour stop for 12 months.
How does the refund guarantee work? ▾
If the campaign doesn't reach ₹4,50,000 by October 5, 2026, every sponsor receives a 100% refund through the same payment rails they paid through. Refunds are processed within 7 working days of the campaign close.
How does the tank auction work? ▾
Live English auction: floor ₹1,00,000, minimum increment ₹10,000, ending October 5, 2026. Anti-sniping: any bid placed in the final 5 minutes extends the auction by 10 minutes. The winner pays their final bid and gets the tank.
Why do Founding prices increase? ▾
Fairness + urgency. The first Founding partner pays ₹50,000, the next pays ₹55,000, and so on — ₹5,000 more per claimed slot. Early believers get the best rate, and the price is always visible live on this page.
What's category exclusivity? ▾
One brand per industry. If an audio brand claims a slot, no other audio brand can join the bike — enforced by the backend at application and bid time. Categories include audio, fintech, D2C, SaaS, AI, food, fashion, health, education, gaming, creator economy, and logistics.
