Straight Answers

What exactly am I buying? ▾
A premium vinyl position on the bike or the rider's gear, a unique QR code with live analytics, category exclusivity, 12 months of documented tour exposure, licensed content from every stop, and a feature in the public case study. One price, no negotiation table.
Why fixed prices instead of an auction? ▾
We researched it. Fixed prices with instant checkout close faster and fairer than bidding wars — auctions delay deals and often close low. One brand, one price, first come. The only rising number is the Founding rate, which rewards brands that move early.
Why does the Founding price rise? ▾
Fairness and urgency. Every Founding slot claimed pushes the next one up by ₹5,000 — live on this page. The first believer pays ₹50,000; the last pays ₹70,000. Early money gets the best rate, forever.
What's category exclusivity? ▾
One brand per industry, enforced by the backend at checkout. If an audio brand owns a slot, no other audio brand can join — the system rejects the payment, not just a handshake. Categories: audio, fintech, D2C, SaaS, AI, food, fashion, health, education, gaming, creator economy, logistics, automotive, and more.
How is exposure measured? ▾
Every position carries a unique QR code. Scans, sponsor page visits, and click-throughs are tracked in real time, plus attendance and photo counts at every tour stop. You get a dashboard — and the final numbers go into the public case study.
What if the target isn't reached? ▾
Every rupee is refunded within 7 working days of October 5, 2026 — through the same rails it came in on. The refund is the guarantee that this only works if it works.
Is this affiliated with Royal Enfield? ▾
No. The Chrome Canvas is an independent public marketing experiment. Royal Enfield makes the machine; the brands make the story.
